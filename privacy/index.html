Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share when you call, email or visit this website.

24/7 Emergency Dispatch

Call Now

The short version

This website does not have forms, accounts or comment sections. The main way you contact us is by phone. That keeps the amount of personal information we handle small. This page explains what we do collect and how third-party services on this site work.

Information you give us

When you call or email us, you decide what to share. We may ask for your name, address and a description of the damage so we can respond. We use that information to answer your call, plan the work and keep records for the job. We do not sell it.

Information collected when you visit

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, browser types and the pages requested. Those logs are used to keep the site running and secure. We do not run user accounts and we do not ask visitors to register.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Third-party services

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which can receive your IP address when the page loads
  • Maps are embedded from Google Maps and may set cookies or receive information according to Google's own policies
  • Phone calls pass through your carrier and ours, and are subject to their practices

We do not control those services. Their policies describe what they collect and how they use it.

Cookies

The site itself does not set cookies for tracking. Embedded maps and fonts may set their own. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you from a job and ask us to correct or remove it where the law allows. Contact us at contact@swanseawaterdamagepros.com or call the number on this page.

Children

This site is meant for adults dealing with property damage. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this page

If we change this policy, we will update the page. The date of the most recent update is November 12, 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the address on our contact page.

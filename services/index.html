Water removal and drying

These services focus on removing water and then drying the structure until readings are normal.

Water Extraction

Contaminated water removal and sanitizing after a sewer or septic backup.

Structural Drying

Not sure which service fits?

Most people are not. If you can describe the water, where it is and where you think it came from, that is enough. We will help you sort out the next step, and we will tell you straight if the problem needs a plumber, a roofer or an electrician first. Our job is the water and what it soaked.

What every job includes

  • A phone conversation about the source, the affected areas and the safety of the space
  • Moisture readings of affected and unaffected materials for comparison
  • Photos before, during and after the work
  • A scope that explains what is being removed, dried or cleaned and why
  • A record of dry readings before equipment comes out

What we hand off to other trades

Water damage work ends where repair work begins. We do not fix pipes, roofs, wiring or appliances, and we do not rebuild walls or install floors. A plumber, roofer, electrician or general contractor handles those jobs, and several of them need a permit from the Swansea building department. We time our drying so those trades are working on a dry building, and we tell you when something we find needs one of them first.

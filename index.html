Local conditions

Why Swansea homes see water losses

Swansea is a bayside town of about 17,375 people in Bristol County, bordered by Mount Hope Bay and cut by river corridors. The town's own hazard mitigation report records floods and storms that shaped its neighborhoods, from the 1938 and 1954 hurricanes to the March 2010 floods that closed schools and cut off emergency access. The southern end of town, the Grove, Touisset, Bushee Road and Cove Street are listed among the flood-prone areas.

Everyday losses are quieter. Older homes with aging supply lines, basements that sit close to the water table and seasonal cottages that freeze in January keep the phones busy. Whatever the cause, the sequence is the same. The water comes out, the structure dries and the damage is documented. Our pages on water extraction, flood damage cleanup and basement water damage explain each stage.

Swansea's building department requires permits for plumbing repair, re-roofing and other work, so the trades who fix the source of a leak have steps of their own to follow. We handle the water and the drying, and we coordinate timing with them.

Documentation for your claim

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance advises homeowners to report a loss promptly and read their policy, since standard policies exclude flood and basement seepage. We keep photos, moisture readings and a record of the work so you have what your carrier asks for.

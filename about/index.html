Who we are

Swansea Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration and mold remediation service for Swansea, Massachusetts and the towns around it. Our office is at 207 Swansea Mall Drive, and our dispatch line is open every hour of the day and night. We keep the site focused on one job, which is helping people who have water where it should not be.

We do not publish made-up reviews, invented credentials or a list of awards, because none of that tells you whether we can help you with a wet basement at midnight. What we can tell you is how we work, what we do and what we do not do, so you can judge for yourself.

What we do

  • Water removal and extraction after leaks, floods and storms
  • Structural drying and dehumidification with measured readings
  • Cleanup after burst pipes, appliance failures and water heater leaks
  • Contaminated water cleanup after sewage backups and floods
  • Mold remediation when moisture has led to growth

The full list is on our services page, and each service has its own page with the causes, the steps and the questions people ask.

What we do not do

We do not fix pipes, replace roofs, do electrical work or rebuild walls and floors. Those jobs belong to plumbers, roofers, electricians and contractors, and several of them require permits from the Swansea Building Department. We dry the building so those trades work on a dry structure, and we tell you when something we find needs one of them first.

We also do not decide insurance coverage, make health claims about mold or promise prices before seeing the damage. We explain the cost factors on the phone and the scope in writing.

Why we write about Swansea the way we do

Our pages draw on public records such as the town's hazard mitigation report, the Swansea Water District's source water assessment, the building department's permit guidance and Massachusetts state agencies. That is why you will read about the Grove, Touisset and Cove Street, the 1938 and 1954 hurricanes and the March 2010 floods. Water damage is local, and knowing which neighborhoods flood and which houses have older plumbing helps us give better advice.

Where we work

Swansea is our home base. We also serve Somerset, Dighton, Rehoboth and Assonet. If you are near the line, call with your address and we will tell you straight whether we can reach you.

What a first call sounds like

Most first calls last a few minutes. We ask where the water is, where it came from and whether anyone is hurt or the power is affected. We ask whether the water is clean, gray or contaminated, because that changes the plan. Then we tell you what to shut off, what to lift and what to photograph, and we explain what happens next.

If the problem is really a plumbing, roofing or electrical one, we say so and suggest you call that trade while we plan the water side.

You will not be pushed into a decision. If you want to think about it or call your insurance carrier first, that is fine. Water does not wait, though, so we will be honest about which steps cannot be put off.

How we keep records

Good records protect you. We take photos before work starts, record moisture readings across the affected materials and compare them with readings from dry parts of your home. Those numbers show when a structure is dry, and they give your carrier something concrete to review. If a dispute comes up later, the record settles what was wet, what was removed and what was done.

How to reach us

Call (508) 286-8971 any time. There are no forms to fill out, which means nothing sits in an inbox while your floor soaks. For non-urgent questions you can email contact@swanseawaterdamagepros.com. Our contact page has the address and a map.

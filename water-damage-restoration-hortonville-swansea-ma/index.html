A historic village on Locust Street

Hortonville is a village in northern Swansea near the Rehoboth border, with Locust Street as its main road. The Hortonville Historic District covers about 122 acres along Locust Street from Oak Street to Hortonville Road and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places on February 16, 1990. The oldest house in the district, at 397 Locust Street, dates to 1736.

A state survey describes Hortonville as a well-preserved grouping of early to mid-19th century cottages. The Swansea Cotton Factory began in Hortonville in 1806, and the Cole River runs through the eastern end of the village. Nearby, our Rehoboth page covers the neighboring town.

Drying an older house with care

We do not claim that a house of any age leaks more than another. What an older house changes is how we work. Plaster, old wood trim and original floors can often be dried in place if the water is clean and the work starts early, so the goal is to measure first and remove only what has to go.

See wall and drywall water damage, hardwood floor water damage and structural drying for how each is handled.

Streams, culverts and a wet month

How drying is planned in a village house

Before anything is cut, moisture meters map which materials are wet and which are dry, and a few readings in an untouched room give the baseline. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run while the readings are repeated, so the plan changes as the structure dries. The aim is to leave original material in place whenever the readings say it can dry.

Tell us on the call how old the wiring and plaster are if you know, whether the basement has a dirt or stone floor, and where the water first appeared. That helps the crew plan the equipment before arriving.

What to do first

  • Close the main valve if you can reach it safely
  • Stay out of a wet basement if the panel or furnace could be in the water
  • Photograph wet plaster, trim and floors before anything is moved
  • Do not pull up old flooring or plaster yourself until the water source is fixed
  • Call us with the street and what the wet material is

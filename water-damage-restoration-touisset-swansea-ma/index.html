Touisset, from summer colony to year-round village

Touisset grew as a summer resort. A state survey of Swansea records that Touisset Park helped build a summer colony around the turn of the 20th century. One real estate guide describes the neighborhood today as Cape Cods, ranch-style homes and colonial-inspired houses, plus some cottages from the summer-resort years, on lots with no sidewalks.

The guide gives a median build year around 1953 and a median lot size near 15,245 square feet. It is a directory source, so treat the figures as approximate.

Which Touisset we serve

What the town report says about Touisset

Swansea's 2017 to 2018 Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness report lists the Touisset and Bushee Road area as vulnerable to flooding. The report is about the town's planning, and it does not describe any single house. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified here, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address.

The same report notes that heavy rain in March 2010 cut off roads, and that some homes lost furnaces and water heaters. We mention it only as a reminder that a wet month can send water into basements well away from the shoreline.

First steps when the power may be affected

  • Do not enter a basement with standing water if the furnace or panel could be wet
  • Shut off the main water valve only if you can reach it safely
  • If an outage has stopped a sump pump, do not wade in to restart it
  • Photograph the damage from a dry spot and lift belongings that are safe to move
  • Call with the street and the nearest cross street

Do not run extension cords through standing water, and do not use an ordinary vacuum on wet floors. If the water came from outside the house, assume it is contaminated until proven otherwise.

Insurance records for a Touisset loss

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance notes that flood is commonly excluded from standard policies. Photos and moisture readings can help document the loss, and we keep both, but we do not decide coverage.
